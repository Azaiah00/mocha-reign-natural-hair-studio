FAQ

Questions, answered

Straight answers from the studio's own policies and menu.

Do new clients need a consultation first?

Yes. Every new client books a free 15-minute consultation before any service. Consultations run on Tuesdays and Wednesdays only, and you book them on Booksy.

Do you take walk-ins?

No. Mocha Reign is appointment only. Book your consultation or service on Booksy, or call (850) 666-0791.

How often should micro locs be maintained?

Micro loc maintenance is booked on a 6 to 8 week window. After week 8, there is an added charge of $10 per week.

How much do micro locs cost?

A Micro Mini install starts at $2,500 and takes about 13 hours. Micro loc maintenance for existing clients starts at $180. Final pricing is confirmed at your consultation.

Do you do kids' locs?

Yes. Kids services are for children 9 and younger: kids starter locs from $95, kids loc maintenance from $60, and maintenance with a style for $75.

Can you repair or reattach locs?

Yes. Loc reattachment starts at $350, takes about 5 hours, and requires a consultation and a deposit.

Do you style locs for weddings and events?

Yes. Tia styles loc updos for weddings, bridal showers, homecoming and other occasions. Start with a free consultation so the style is planned before the day.

Where is Mocha Reign and when is it open?

The studio is at 1533-4 South Monroe Street, Tallahassee, FL 32301. Google lists Tuesday to Friday 8:30 AM to 8:30 PM and Saturday 8:30 AM to 4:30 PM, closed Sunday and Monday. Appointments only.
