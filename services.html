Services FAQ

Pricing questions

What does the + after a price mean?

The + means the price is a starting price, as listed on Booksy. Length, density and the style you choose can change the final price, which is confirmed at your consultation.

Do I have to book a consultation first?

Yes, if you are a new client. The consultation is free, takes 15 minutes, and is available on Tuesdays and Wednesdays only.

What happens if I go past 8 weeks between micro loc appointments?

Micro loc maintenance is booked on a 6 to 8 week window. After week 8, $10 is added for each additional week.

What ages are kids services for?

Kids services are for children 9 and younger.
