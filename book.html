Booking FAQ

Booking questions

How do I book?

All appointments are booked on Booksy under Mocha Reign Studios. New clients book the free 15-minute consultation first, on a Tuesday or Wednesday.

Can I just walk in?

No. Mocha Reign does not take walk-ins. Every visit is by appointment.

Can I call instead?

Yes. Call the studio at (850) 666-0791.

Is there parking?

Yes. The studio lists parking, credit card payments, Wi-Fi and accessible entry.

Where exactly is the studio?

1533-4 South Monroe Street, Tallahassee, FL 32301.

Which hours are listed?
