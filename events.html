How to book an occasion

Plan it first.

  1. 1

    Free consultation

    Book a 15-minute consult on a Tuesday or Wednesday. Talk through the style, the date and the timing.

  2. 2

    Send the details

    For a bridal party or a group, use the inquiry form so the studio knows the date, the event and how many people.

  3. 3

    Reserve your chair

    Book the styling appointment on Booksy once the plan is set.

What to bring

  • Reference photos of the style you love, saved on your phone.
  • Your event date and timing, including when you need to be ready.
  • Your headcount if others in the party want their hair done.
  • Accessories you plan to wear, such as cuffs, shells, pins or a veil.

Events FAQ

Occasion questions

How far ahead should I book wedding or event hair?

Book your free consultation as early as you can so the style is planned and your appointment is on the book. Consultations run on Tuesdays and Wednesdays.

Can Mocha Reign style a whole wedding party?

Send the inquiry form with the number of people and the date, and the studio will get back to you about availability and planning.

Does Tia travel for events?

Tia describes herself as a traveling stylist. Mention your venue in the inquiry form or at your consultation to ask about travel.

How much does event styling cost?

Loc styling starts at $35+ for style only and $55+ for loc knots on the Booksy menu. Event pricing depends on the style, the number of people and timing, and is quoted after your consultation.
