The studio

A natural hair & body Art studio.

Mocha Reign is Tia “MochaReign” Simmons's studio on South Monroe Street, Tallahassee. Locs are the medium. Every head is treated like a commission.

Owner and loc artist

Tia “MochaReign” Simmons

“Owner of MochaReign Natural hair studios/ @mochareignmgmt Creator of MochaReign hair care products! / Free Consultations / Traveling stylist!”

That is Tia in her own words, from her Instagram bio. She calls the studio “a natural hair & body Art studio,” and the work backs it up: sculpted loc updos, micro locs, wicks, loc hawks and color, with a book of clients who keep coming back.

Mocha Reign has been in business in Tallahassee since 2010. The studio moved from 1815 South Adams Street to its current home at 1533-4 South Monroe Street, and it holds a 5.0 rating from 670 reviews on Booksy.

Tia is also a traveling stylist. If you need your locs styled somewhere other than the studio, ask about it at your consultation or call (850) 666-0791.

How the studio works

Consult. Create. Maintain.

The way Mocha Reign runs its book, straight from the studio's own policies.

Consult first

Every new client starts with a free 15-minute consultation on a Tuesday or Wednesday. Your hair is assessed before a price is set.

Appointment only

No walk-ins. Your time in the chair is reserved for you, from a 30-minute style to a 13-hour micro install.

A maintenance rhythm

Locs thrive on schedule. Micro locs are maintained every 6 to 8 weeks, with +$10 per week after week 8.
